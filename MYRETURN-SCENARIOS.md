# My return - individual income-tax scenarios (AY 2026-27)

What an individual's return can hold, where each figure comes from, and what SimplyFile's My return does with it.
Written 2026-10-06 from the department's AY 2026-27 forms and the Finance Act 2026. Status: **done** / **part** / **todo**.

## The documents - who gives them and where to get them

| Document | Issued by | Where to get it | What it gives the return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Form 16 (Part A + B) | Employer (TRACES) | Employer's HR / payroll, by 15 June | Salary, exemptions, standard deduction, 80C...80CCD(2) declared, TDS on salary |
| Form 16A | Bank / payer (TRACES) | The bank or client that deducted | TDS on interest, fees, rent (also in 26AS) |
| Form 26AS (Annual Tax Statement) | Income Tax Department (TRACES) | incometax.gov.in > e-File > Income Tax Returns > View Form 26AS > TRACES | Every TDS / TCS credited to your PAN, refunds; tax payments up to FY 2022-23 |
| AIS (Annual Information Statement) | Income Tax Department | incometax.gov.in > Services > AIS (PDF or JSON; PDF password = PAN in small letters + date of birth DDMMYYYY) | Salary, interest, dividends, sale of shares / funds / property, rent, business receipts, GST turnover, **tax payments (advance, self-assessment) from FY 2023-24** |
| TIS (Taxpayer Information Summary) | Income Tax Department | Same page as the AIS | The AIS totalled per category (the figures the department will use) |
| Pre-filled JSON | Income Tax Department | incometax.gov.in > e-File > Income Tax Returns > Download Pre-Filled Data | Who you are, banks, salary per employer, TDS, taxes paid, interest |
| Last year's / filed return (JSON) | You (from the e-filing portal) | incometax.gov.in > e-File > Income Tax Returns > View Filed Returns > Download JSON | Your date of birth, the regime used, losses carried forward, what was filed |
| Bank statement | Your bank | Net banking > Statements (PDF / Excel) | Receipts of a profession or business with no TDS, interest not in the AIS |
| GST returns (GSTR-1 / 3B, annual) | GST portal | gst.gov.in > Returns > View filed returns | Turnover of a GST-registered business (must agree with the receipts shown) |
| Capital gains statement | Broker / CAMS / KFintech | Broker back office (Tax P&L), CAMS / KFintech consolidated statement (CSV / Excel - read on Gather) | Each sale: date, sale value, cost, grandfathered value (112A), STT |
| Last year's filed return (JSON) | You (from the e-filing portal) | incometax.gov.in > e-File > Income Tax Returns > View Filed Returns > AY before this one > Download JSON | Schedule CFL: the losses still to carry forward (read on Gather, "Fill from last year's return") |
| Sale deed / buyer's Form 16B | Buyer (TRACES, 26QB) | The buyer; the AIS shows TDS-194IA and SFT-012 | Sale price, stamp-duty value, the buyer's PAN and TDS |
| Home loan interest certificate | Lender | Lender's portal | Interest (24(b)) and principal (80C) |
| Rent receipts / agreement | Landlord | - | HRA claim (old regime) |
| Investment proofs | LIC, PPF, ELSS, NPS, insurer | Each provider | 80C, 80CCD(1B), 80D, 80E, 80G |
| Advance / self-assessment tax challans (280) | Bank / e-Pay Tax | incometax.gov.in > e-File > e-Pay Tax > Payment history | Tax you paid yourself (also in the AIS Part B3) |

## The way in: New filing (wizard) - 2026-10-11

Owner, after a client demo: "the flow is not intuitive - it must be a wizard". **New filing** (Filing menu, first; Start a new filing on Clients & years, My return and the Start here card) puts four steps in front of My return - **done**:
1. *What you're filing*: who (Myself on a self-filer's desk; a client or a family HUF in a firm - chip wizard.settings who, wizard.offered) and what applies - salary, a second employer, a house, interest, shares and funds sold, land or a house sold, crypto, a business or profession, foreign income or assets, days abroad, salary arrears, last year's losses, a revised or updated return (chip wizard.scenarios: plain words, the section beneath; an HUF is offered no salary / arrears). The likely form in one line - chip wizard.form on myreturn.form (no second engine).
2. *Documents you'll need*: chip wizard.docs (each with what it is and where to get it), needed / good to have by wizard.doc_need; what is typed waits for Review.
3. *Upload*: one drop zone, several files; each read by myreturn_read.read (the same readers), who it names by myreturn.who or the text (chip myreturn.sniff: 26AS / AIS / CAMS headers). A card per file; a file not read says **Not recognised** and stays out; another PAN's document stays out; a locked AIS opens by itself once the date of birth is given. The client is made from the PAN + name (or found by PAN) and every waiting document goes into the return (myreturn.add_doc, the same "already here?" question). The popup asks only what is missing or does not agree (chip wizard.asks: whose PAN when two, a PAN, the PAN's kind against the return chosen, name, an HUF's name, date of birth, the Karta's name and PAN, residence). The hints (chip wizard.prompts): AIS fund / share sales without a capital-gains statement, 194IA in 26AS / AIS without a property sale, a salary TAN without its Form 16, 194S crypto, 194J / 194C fees without a business, a business without GSTR-3B, losses without last year's ITR, foreign assets / arrears to enter.
4. *Review*: what was understood, what is missing, and Continue in My return at the step chip wizard.next_step names (Gather / Review / Check / Choose).
Saved on the server per filing (sf_wizard/<firm>/<id>), listed under *Carry on where you left off*; Discard drops only the wizard's progress. Tests: tools/e2e_wizard.py (HUF, salaried + capital gains, Not recognised, the popup, the tenant wall). Demo documents: tools/showcase/make_myreturn_demo.py -> wizard-sal/, wizard-huf/, wizard-misc/.

Later on 2026-10-11 the wizard covers every return SimplyFy files - people, HUFs, firms, LLPs, AOP / BOI, companies (trusts: not yet) - with one worked example and film per path: see **FILING-PATHS.md**.

## Scenarios

### Salary and pension
- One employer, Form 16 - **done** (TRACES Form 16 read in layout, 80CCD(2) to its own row, TDS checked against 26AS)
- The employer's address in ITR-2 Schedule S - **done 2026-10-07** (Form 16 Part A's employer block, left column when laid out in two: address, city, PIN; the state from its name, else guessed from the PIN - chip myreturn.read state_names / pin_states)
- Two or more employers in the year (two Form 16s; double standard deduction is a common mistake) - **done** (one standard deduction; 2026-10-08: each Form 16's Chapter VI-A added up, the caps once, and an amber check (chip myreturn.check_employers) - each employer took off the standard deduction, the return takes it once; 80C declared to both over 1.5 lakh)
- A second Form 16 from the same employer (same TAN) - **done 2026-10-08** (chip myreturn.doc_clash, settings tan_kinds): asked first, the "already here" dialog - **replace** (a revised Form 16; the first kept in History) or **keep both** (two spells with one employer - both salaries count). Another TAN is a second employer: kept, no question.
- Exempt allowances: HRA (old regime, metro / non-metro), LTA, gratuity, leave encashment - **done** (from Form 16; HRA worked out from rent)
- Professional tax, standard deduction (75,000 new / 50,000 old) - **done**
- Family pension (1/3 or 15,000 / 25,000) - **done**
- Arrears and relief under section 89 (Form 10E) - **done 2026-10-07** (wave 3, chips myreturn.slabs_by_year / tax_of_year / arrear_row / relief_89): each year the arrears belong to typed with that year's total income and regime (blank: old before FY 2023-24, new from it); relief = (tax this year with the arrears - without) - sum of (that year's tax with - without), nil when negative; each earlier year at its own slabs, 87A (marginal relief in the new regime from FY 2023-24) and cess (3% to 2017-18, 4% after), FY 2015-16 on, new regime from FY 2020-21. **Law decision**: s.89 relief IS available in the new regime - s.115BAC(2) takes away exemptions and deductions, not a relief from tax. Not for an HUF, needs salary (ITR-2 rules 516, 542). ITR-1 / ITR-4 Section89, ITR-2 / ITR-3 Part B-TTI TaxRelief.Section89; NetTaxLiability after it; Form 10E reminder. 2026-10-08: each earlier year's tax by chip myreturn.year_tax - that year's **surcharge with marginal relief** (12% / 15% over 1 crore to FY 2016-17; 10% / 15% over 50 lakh / 1 crore from FY 2017-18; 25% / 37% over 2 / 5 crore from FY 2019-20, the new regime's top 25% from FY 2023-24) - a year over 50 lakh is worked out, not left to a CA.
- Salary from abroad / foreign employer - **done 2026-10-07** (a resident's foreign income, below: Schedule S row with state 99 and the ZIP code, nature OTH)

### House property
- Self-occupied (interest up to 2 lakh, old regime) - **done**
- Let out (rent, municipal tax, 30%, interest) and the loss set off against salary up to 2 lakh (CYLA) - **done**
- Co-owned (share percent, s.26) - **done 2026-10-07** (rent, municipal tax and loan interest each by the share; the 30% on the share's annual value; the 2 lakh self-occupied cap per co-owner on their share; Schedule HP PropCoOwnedFlg, AsseseeShareProperty, CoOwners, BalanceALV = the whole property's, AnnualOfPropOwned = the share - ITR-1, ITR-2, ITR-4)
- Pre-construction interest (1/5 for 5 years) - **done 2026-10-07** (s.24(b) Explanation: the total and the day the house was bought / finished; a fifth in each of five years from that year, added to the year's interest - inside the 2 lakh cap for self-occupied (old regime), by the share for a co-owner; chips myreturn.pre_construction, myreturn.house); 80EE / 80EEA - **done** (see deductions)
- House-property loss carried forward / brought forward - **done 2026-10-07** (old regime: what the 2 lakh set-off (CYLA) leaves goes forward; new regime: the whole let-out loss goes forward (no set-off against other heads, s.115BAC(2)); brought forward (s.71B, 8 years) only against house income - Schedule BFLA HP, Schedule CFL; a belated return still carries it, s.80 not naming 71B)

### Other sources
- Savings interest (80TTA 10,000 / 80TTB 50,000 for seniors) - **done**
- FD / RD interest (AIS SFT-016, 26AS 194A) - **done**
- Dividends (every SFT-015 row) - **done**
- Interest on income-tax refund - **done**
- Lottery, crossword, races, card games, betting (115BB) and online games (115BBJ), 30% - **done 2026-10-07** (both regimes; no basic exemption, no 87A rebate on their tax - they still count in total income for the 87A limit - no Chapter VI-A, no house loss set off against them; surcharge at the full rate; ITR-2 Schedule OS rows and date range (from the date typed), Schedule SI 5BB / 5BBJ; AIS TDS-194B / 194BB / 194BA and 26AS 194B / 194BB / 194BA read as winnings)
- Crypto / virtual digital assets (115BBH), 30% - **done 2026-10-07** (each transfer typed: sale less cost, nothing else; a loss counts for nothing and is not carried forward; ITR-2 Schedule VDA, Schedule CG IncmFromVDATrnsf and date range, Part B-TI CapGains30Per115BBH, Schedule SI 5BBH; the AIS's TDS-194S sale value is shown beside the rows)
- Gifts (56(2)(x)) - **done 2026-10-07** (each gift typed: from whom, relation, occasion, money / land-building / movable, value, price paid. From a relative (the Explanation's list, chip myreturn.gift_rules), on marriage, by will / inheritance, in contemplation of death - nothing. Sums and movable property: taxable when more than 50,000 in the year together - all of it; bought for less - the shortfall when more than 50,000 together; land / building each on its own: free - the stamp value over 50,000, for less - the shortfall over the higher of 50,000 and 10% of the price. ITR-2 Schedule OS 1d (i)-(v); ITR-1 / ITR-4 "any other" (a gift alone stays ITR-1))

### Capital gains (wave 3, 2026-10-07 - chips myreturn.cg_rules / cg_row / cg_exempt / cg_choice / waterfall / loss_age / cii / fy_of)

- 111A short-term (A2, section code 1A) and other short-term assets (A5) - **done** (aggregated per rate)
- Debt funds - **done** (bought from 1-4-2023: s.50AA short-term at slab rates whatever the holding; bought before and held over 24 months: 112 at 12.5% without indexation; under 24 months: slab)
- Land / building sold - **done**: s.50C (stamp-duty value taken when more than 110% of the price), cost + improvement (each indexed by chip myreturn.cii, CII 2001-02 = 100 ... 2025-26 = 376; bought before 1-4-2001: FMV on that day, base 2001-02), transfer expenses; for a resident individual / HUF and land / building bought before 23-07-2024 both computations - 12.5% without indexation and 20% with it - shown on Review, the lower used: the gain is shown without indexation and the excess (TaxSec1121a - TaxSec1121aiiB = ExcessAmtSec1121a, TotalExcessTax) comes off the 112 tax (Schedule SI code 21) - chip myreturn.tax excess_112. Short-term property (A1) at slab rates. The buyer (name, PAN, address, PIN) in TrnsfImmblPrprty. **Law decision**: the choice is made per property on its own gain after the exemption (the proviso's words); the basic-exemption shortfall is applied by myreturn.tax to the 12.5% gain only.

- Exemptions 54 / 54EC / 54F with the Capital Gains Account Scheme - **done** (54: a house sold, up to the new house's cost + CGAS deposit, at most 10 crore counted; 54EC: land / building, bonds up to 50 lakh in the year (the room shrinks sale by sale); 54F: any other long-term asset, gain x new house's cost (10 crore cap) / net consideration, all of it when the cost is as much, refused with more than one other house owned; worked out on both computations of a property. Schedule CG B1 ExemptionOrDednUs54, 112A / other assets DeductionUs54F, Table D with the CGAS deposit, date, account, IFSC). 2026-10-08: **several exemptions on one sale** (54 + 54EC on a house, 54F + 54EC on land; up to 4 - knob cg_rules ex_max): each against the gain, then each in the order typed up to the gain still left (chip myreturn.waterfall), on both computations; Table D one claim per section, Schedule CG B1 ExemptionOrDednUs54Dtls one line per section. **Time limits checked** (chip myreturn.cg_window): 54 / 54F house bought 1 year before - 2 years after the transfer, built within 3; 54EC bonds within 6 months after; the CGAS deposit by the s.139(1) due date (31 July - My return files no audit cases); out of time = not counted and said so; a date not typed is taken as in time and asked for. **part**: the 54 two-house option (gain up to 2 crore, once) and 54B (agricultural land) are not offered.
