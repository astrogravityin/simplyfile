R1

Which ITR form (1, 2, 3 or 4), and why

Law Rule 12, Income-tax Rules 1962; the ITR forms notified for AY 2026-27

Picks ITR-1, ITR-2, ITR-3 or ITR-4 from what the return holds: residence, total income, houses, capital gains, farm income, directorship, unlisted shares, foreign assets, losses carried, and presumptive business income. The first reason that applies is shown to the person.

Worked examples - the engine is tested on each

    gives form ITR-1
    why “ITR-1 (Sahaj) fits: resident, income up to 50 lakh, salary, up to two houses and other sources.”
  2. Given total = 14,00,000; ltcg_112a = 90,000
    gives form ITR-1
    why “ITR-1 (Sahaj) fits: resident, income up to 50 lakh, salary, up to two houses and other sources.”
  3. Given total = 14,00,000; ltcg_112a = 2,00,000
    gives form ITR-2
    why “ITR-2, because of capital gains beyond 112A gains of 1.25 lakh.”

    gives form ITR-2
    why “ITR-2, because your total income is above 50 lakh.”
  5. Given resident = no
    gives form ITR-2
    why “Not resident and ordinarily resident (a non-resident or RNOR): ITR-1 and ITR-4 are for residents only - ITR-2.”
